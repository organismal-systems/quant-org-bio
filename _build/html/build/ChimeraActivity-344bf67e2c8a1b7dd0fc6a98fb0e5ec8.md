# 🛼 Morphologies that meet the challenges of early larval swimming
The [preceding discussion](./SizeSwim.md) outlined the hypothesis that, to survive through development and successfully metamorphose into juveniles, many or most early stage marine invertebrate larvae must satisfy functional requirements for swimming performance.
Specifically, two hypothetical "performance standards" were proposed: swimming speed and stability.
The benefits and costs of swimming are complex, poorly understood and highly variable, making it difficult to critically assess tradeoffs implied by this hypothesis, beyond the type of observations and analysis that first motivated them.
