# ️💡️  A discrete-time model of cholera immune period
[Koelle et al. (2005)](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature03820) developed a model in which they tried to simultaneously quantify several epidemiological and environmental factors affecting cholera incidence in a focal site, Matlab, Bangladesh.
Their paper was a huge parameter-fitting exercise, which is quite complicated, but the model they used to simulate cholera outbreaks is relatively simple.




In these Study Questions, we will use a recreation of their model in the worksheet cholera4.sws to gain insights into the effectiveness of possible cholera mitigation strategies.
