# 📖 Minimum habitat size in the KISS model: Back-of-the-envelope estimates

The premise of the KISS model is that an isolated patch of good habitat lies surrounded by an infinate expanse of hostile non-habitat.
The habitat patch support survival and reproduction for a resident population.
Individuals in this population move randomly, which is reflected at the population level as a diffusion (analgous to mass diffusion, heat conduction, *etc*.).
However, any individuals that encounter the edge of the habitat and drift into non-habitat die immediately and are lost to the population.
