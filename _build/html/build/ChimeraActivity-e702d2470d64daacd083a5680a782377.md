# 🛼 Morphologies that meet the challenges of early larval swimming
The model in this Unit presents an opportunity to quantitatively assess the hypotheses that their is, for many or most early stage marine invertebrate larvae, are functional requirement for swimming performance meeting minimal "standards".
Specifically, two hypothetical requirements were suggested in the [preceding discussion](./SizeSwim.md):
1. **Swimming speed**: The upward swimming speed of early stage larvae in still water meet or exceed a minimum speed, $V_{min}$.
2. **Stability**: Early stage larvae must be capable of maintaining vertical orientation in the shear and turbulence characteristic of the habitats in which they undergo development.

The observations and analysis of [](doi:10.1093/icb/icq090) suggested that the minimum speed performance requirement is in the vicinity of $350 \frac{\mu m}{s}$.
